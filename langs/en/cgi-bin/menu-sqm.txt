The WUI menu is assembled from every /var/ipfire/menu.d/*.menu file
(header.pl evals them in glob order).

This overlay installs var/ipfire/menu.d/41-sqm.menu, which adds SQM under
Services immediately after Quality of Service (sort key 55.sqm vs 50.qos).

It does not patch 40-services.menu, so a core update that replaces stock
menu files will not wipe the SQM entry unless it also deletes extra files
in menu.d.
